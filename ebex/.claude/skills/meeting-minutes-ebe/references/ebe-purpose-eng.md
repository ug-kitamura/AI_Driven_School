# **Our Purpose**

## **Why does our organization exist?**

> "Empower every associate to be efficient"
> (=For all associates in engineering area to be able to act independently and efficiently.)

This is the purpose of VM/EBE-JP. VM/EBE-JP exists to achieve this purpose.

---

# **Our Strategies**

## **#1: Individual focus**

### **How to realize our purpose?**
With a strong focus on individuals, we work on items (1) to (5) with the aim of "fostering each individual's capability to accomplish tasks (competency) and develop problem-solving skills (solutions)". This approach will contribute to the fulfillment of VM/EBE-JP's purpose.
We are using **flower** logo as a metaphor symbolizing 'the individual' within the engineering area.

| No. | Concept | Description |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| (1) | **Soil** | This represents the foundation for individual growth and serves as the "source of PMT (Process, Method, Tool) information." It is the wellspring of our daily work. |
| (2) | **Plant Root** | PMT information is crucial for an individual's development. This signifies the interface, or "accessibility (efficiency)," between PMT information and the individual. |
| (3) | **Sunshine Management** | "Making PMT information, which exists as knowledge, practically usable and actionable through training" is considered photosynthesis, represented by Sunshine Management. |
| (4) | **Pouring Water** | This represents "coaching that encourages growth in a way tailored to each individual's state, provides insights, and enables the more effective and efficient utilization of PMT information." |
| (5) | **Leaf** | This is "the individual's will to actively adopt, utilize, and practice the knowledge gained from training and coaching." It is the leaf itself, the main player in producing energy. We support individuals to foster a positive movement in this regard. |

## **#2: Environment focus**

### **How to realize our purpose?**
Next, we focus on the "environment". Through improvements and contributions to items (6) to (10), our goal is to "create an environment where each individual can operate effectively and efficiently." This will lead to the realization of VM/EBE-JP's purpose.

| No. | Concept | Description |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| (6) | **Maintain right content PMT** | We will establish an environment to ensure that PMT information remains consistently accurate and meaningful. |
| (7) | **Keep healthy Efficiency** | We focus on "healthy efficiency" generated with compliance, striving to create an environment that does not undermine the organization's overall efficiency. |
| (8) | **Future-proof** | We will focus on proactive measures against future uncertainties (business needs, market trends, etc.) and establish an environment that allows for early preparation. |
| (9) | **We are One VM** | Across all VM product areas, we will foster inter-organizational transparency and establish an environment where efficiency can be deployed horizontally. |
| (10)| **We belong to EA** | Based on fairness, transparency, and collaboration, we aim to create an environment that encourages proactive information sharing and the horizontal deployment of good practices within EA. |
